# About Me

My name is Mary Bireley and I am currently in my last year at Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College earning my Master of Science in Public Policy and Management. I currently also work at the Churchill Borough as their Community Engagement and Project Specialist. I am passionate about the intersection of public policy and community engagement and service. 

After I graduate, I hope to work in local government or the non-profit sector to help advocate for effective public policy that is implemented in tandem with communities to better serve their needs. 

# What I Hope to Learn

I hope to learn how to better visualize and communicate complex policy data so it is accessible to everyone, from data analysts to policymakers to community members.
